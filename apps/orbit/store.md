Pong for a round screen. A ball bounces around inside the watch face, and your paddle rides the ring at its edge.
Turn the crown to slide the paddle round and keep the ball in, or tap the side of the screen you want the paddle to
head for. Where the ball lands on the paddle tilts the bounce, so you can aim it.

Every bounce is a point. The ball speeds up and the paddle gets shorter as the score climbs. Let the ball slip past and
the run is over: tap to go again. Your best score is kept on the watch.

Press the crown or swipe right to leave.

## Requirements

The [patched firmware](/firmware) and the [App Loader](/firmware). Open it from the **Apps** row at the end of the
watch's app list (list style, not the waterfall).
