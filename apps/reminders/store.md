Your iPhone's open reminders on the watch. Overdue ones come first in red, then today's, then the next days and the
ones without a date, each with its list's colour as a dot. The crown scrolls. Tap one and confirm **Done** to tick it
off on the phone: the watch buzzes once and the row goes.

Long-press **Reminders** in Apps and choose **Add widget** for a card next to the watch face with how many are due
today and the next one.

The watch asks the phone when you open the app and every 3 minutes while it's open, and keeps the last list, so it
opens at once without the phone. A reminder ticked off while the phone is away is sent when it's back.

Titles stay on the phone by default and the watch shows "Reminder" and the time. To see them, turn on "Show text on
the watch" for Reminders in FMC Companion: anyone near enough to the watch's Bluetooth could read them too.

The first time, the watch says "Allow Reminders in FMC Companion on your phone": open FMC Companion, Apps, Reminders,
and allow it (iOS then asks for access to your reminders).

## Requirements

- An **iPhone** with **FMC Companion**, paired with the watch. Android has no system reminders other apps can read,
  so on Android the app only says "Reminders need an iPhone".
- The [patched firmware](/firmware) with the channel key (2026-11-13 or later) and the [App Loader](/firmware)
  (2026-11-15 or later for the widget).
