A year of days as a grid of squares, in black and white.

- **Tap a day** to cycle its level: clear, low, ok, good, great. The square fills in four steps up to white, so a month reads at a glance.
- **Crown** flips between the months of the current year.
- A whole year of levels fits in the app's storage, so the grid is still there next time.

Nothing leaves the watch and no phone is needed. It needs the clock, so set the time (the phone app syncs it) before
the first launch.
