The weather where your phone is, in three pages you turn with the crown or a swipe:

- **Now**: the place, the temperature, the condition, today's high and low, wind and humidity.
- **Hours**: the next 12 hours with the temperature and, when it is likely, the chance of rain or snow.
- **Days**: the week ahead, each day with its low, its high and a bar that shows where they sit in the week.

Long-press **Weather** in Apps and choose **Add page** to keep the Now screen one swipe from the watch face, or
**Add widget** for a card with the temperature, the condition and today's high and low.

The watch asks the phone when you open the app and every 15 minutes while it's shown, and keeps the last answer, so
it opens at once without the phone and says how old the weather is. Temperatures are in °C.

The first time, the watch says "Allow Weather in FMC Companion on your phone": open FMC Companion, Apps, Weather, and
allow it (the phone then asks for your location). The watch gets the weather and the name of the place, never where
you are.

## Requirements

- A phone with **FMC Companion** (iOS or Android), paired with the watch. The hours need FMC Companion from
  2026-11-20 or later; with an older one the Hours page asks you to update it and the rest works.
- The [patched firmware](/firmware) with the channel key (2026-11-13 or later) and the [App Loader](/firmware)
  (2026-11-15 or later for the page and the widget).
