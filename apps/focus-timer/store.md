A focus session timer in black and white.

- **Crown** sets the length: one click is one minute, 1 to 99.
- **Tap Start** to begin. The white ring empties as the session runs and the countdown sits in the middle.
- **Tap Pause** to stop; what is left is kept, so the next tap resumes from there.
- **Three motor pulses** (250 ms each) mark the end. The button then reads **Restart**.

The chosen length is saved, so it is still there the next time the app opens. Nothing else is stored and nothing
leaves the watch; it needs no phone.
