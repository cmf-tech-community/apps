Silent tactile metronome for CMF Watch Pro 2 with a native LVGL interface.

### Features
- **Tactile Rhythm:** Distinct vibration accent on the first beat of every bar.
- **Tap Tempo:** Tap the beat directly on screen to match any song's tempo.
- **Crown & Touch Control:** Adjust BPM with the crown (`±1`) or quick `±5` pills.
- **Customizable:** Configure time signatures (`1/4` to `8/4`) and vibration strength (`20%` to `100%`).
- **Always-On Session:** Keeps the screen and motor active during practice.

## Requirements

The [patched firmware](/firmware) and the [App Loader](/firmware).
